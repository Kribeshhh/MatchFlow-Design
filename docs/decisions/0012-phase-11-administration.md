# ADR 0012 — Platform administration and moderation

Accepted 2026-11-04 before implementation. User approved mandatory staff TOTP with one-use recovery codes, moderators enforcing ordinary-user/content cases, administrators controlling staff/ownership/operations, and configurable retention defaults (90 days resolved support, 365 days moderation/security/API evidence) with legal holds. Stop after Phase 11; no commercial features.

Platform authority stays separate from organizer authority. Every admin endpoint requires verified active platform membership, enrolled TOTP and a recent session-bound second-factor proof; sensitive mutations also require recent primary reauthentication. OTPAuth implements RFC 6238; encrypted factor secrets use standard Node authenticated encryption with a dedicated externally configured key. Codes are throttled, replay-protected, and recovery codes are random/hash-only/single-use. Enrollment requires recent authentication; existing sessions/users survive migration but receive no implicit staff elevation. Missing key fails closed. No password-reset or OAuth flow bypasses the staff factor gate.

Reports/support cases are authenticated, rate-limited, bounded plain text. Ordinary report targets must already be visible to the reporter; separately authenticated recent staff may initiate private-resource cases through /admin/cases. case IDs never confer access. Reporters see only their own submissions and public resolution; internal reasons/history are staff-only. Moderation operations recheck current role and factor/session inside serializable transactions. Moderators cannot enforce against platform staff or transfer ownership. Admins cannot suspend/demote the last active administrator. Suspension revokes sessions; restoration does not resurrect sessions or privileges.

Content restriction preserves competition records while removing abusive public text and preventing ordinary edits. Restricted evidence stores prior content under staff-only access. Restoration is explicit and audited, never silently overwrites subsequent edits. Organization transfers require an existing verified consenting member and administrator reason, revoke pending offers/access grants as appropriate and do not expose another tenant's records through ordinary routes. Result review retains existing dispute, rollback preview, reason and replay semantics; platform authority is narrowly checked in the correction path, not a global tournament ownership bypass.

AuditEvent remains fixed actions/identifiers with no client mutation endpoints. Moderation evidence is separate, access-controlled, versioned and subject to retention/legal holds. No ordinary organization role can read the platform queue. Operational health/usage aggregates are actual database values; unavailable external monitors are identified. Self-service data export excludes secrets and other users' personal information. Existing anonymizing deletion retains tournament history; moderation evidence may be held under documented retention, not published.

Cleanup is an explicit operator command with dry-run default, configured periods and hold checks; it does not delete tournament results. Production backups, restore drills, incident response, escalation contacts and jurisdiction/privacy review are release gates. No claims of legal compliance or external monitoring configuration arise from repository code.

OTP implementation reference: https://github.com/hectorm/otpauth and https://hectorm.github.io/otpauth/classes/TOTP.html (validation delta, bounded window and throttling).
